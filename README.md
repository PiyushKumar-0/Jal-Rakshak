# 💧 JalRakshak AI

### AI-Powered Water Intelligence & Community Response Platform

> **Turning citizen water reports into explainable risk signals, actionable priorities, and verified resolutions.**

JalRakshak AI is an **offline-first, multilingual water intelligence platform** designed to help citizens, Panchayats, field teams, and district authorities identify and respond to emerging water-related problems.

The platform combines **AI-powered issue classification, risk analysis, geospatial insights, citizen reporting, field verification, and Panchayat-level dashboards** into one unified system.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Water-related problems in rural and semi-urban areas are often reported late, handled manually, or remain disconnected across different stakeholders.

Common challenges include:

- 💧 Contaminated or unsafe water sources
- 🚰 Broken hand pumps and pipelines
- 🦠 Potential water-borne disease risks
- 🌧️ Flooding and waterlogging
- 📍 Lack of centralized location-based information
- 📱 Limited connectivity in rural areas
- 📝 Manual reporting and follow-up
- ⏳ Delayed response to high-risk issues
- 🔄 Poor visibility into whether reported problems were actually resolved

There is a need for a system that can convert scattered citizen reports into **actionable intelligence for local authorities**.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**JalRakshak AI** creates a complete reporting-to-resolution workflow.

### Citizen → AI → Panchayat → Field Team → Verification → Resolution

Citizens can report water-related problems using **text, voice, or photographs**.

The system processes these reports and generates:

- Issue classification
- Risk score
- Risk level
- Explainable risk factors
- Confidence level
- Location-based insights
- Priority recommendations

Panchayat officials can then monitor emerging hotspots, assign tasks to field teams, track progress, and verify resolutions.

# ✨ Key Features

## 👥 Citizen Reporting

Citizens can report water-related issues from their location.

### Supported reporting methods

- 📝 Text reports
- 🎙️ Voice-based reporting
- 📷 Photo-based evidence
- 📍 Location-based reporting
- 🌐 Multilingual interaction
- 📱 Offline-first reporting

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Risk Analysis

JalRakshak AI analyzes citizen reports and generates explainable risk signals.

The system can identify:

- Issue category
- Severity
- Potential risk
- Priority
- Confidence
- Contributing factors

Instead of simply showing an AI-generated score, the platform aims to explain **why a report has been classified as high, medium, or low risk.**

---

## 🗺️ Water Risk Hotspots

Multiple reports can be aggregated geographically to identify areas where problems are increasing.

Authorities can monitor:

- High-risk locations
- Repeated complaints
- Emerging hotspots
- Clustered incidents
- Localized water issues

This helps shift the approach from **reactive complaint handling to proactive intervention.**

---

# 🏛️ Panchayat Dashboard

The Panchayat dashboard provides a centralized view of reported water issues.

Officials can:

- View incoming reports
- Monitor risk levels
- Identify priority cases
- View location-based issues
- Assign tasks
- Track field activities
- Monitor resolution status

---

# 👷 Field Team Workflow

Once an issue is assigned, field teams can inspect the reported location.

They can:

1. Receive assigned task
2. Visit the reported location
3. Inspect the issue
4. Upload observations/evidence
5. Update task status
6. Mark the issue for resolution
7. Submit verification information

This creates a traceable workflow from **report → inspection → action → verification**.
